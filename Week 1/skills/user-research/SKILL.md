# User Research

## Goal
Decisions based on evidence from real users, not opinions.

## Always do
1. **Start with a research question and a decision** it will inform ("Should we build X for Y?").
2. **Pick the method for the question**: interviews (why), usability tests (can they), surveys (how many), analytics (what happened), competitive analysis (what exists).
3. **Recruit the right people**: real target users; 5–8 per segment for interviews/usability tests.
4. **Neutral questions**: ask about past behavior ("Tell me about the last time you…"), never leading or hypothetical ("Would you use…?").
5. **Jobs-to-be-done** framing: "When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome]."
6. **Synthesize**: affinity map → themes → insights (with evidence count and quotes) → recommendations.
7. **Separate observation from interpretation**.
8. **Personas** only from real data; include goals, pains, context — not demographics trivia.
9. **Competitive analysis**: table of competitors × features, pricing, positioning, gaps.
10. **Consent and privacy**: informed consent; anonymize participants.

## Output templates
- Research plan: question, decision, method, participants, script, timeline.
- Findings: insight – evidence (n/N, quote) – implication – recommendation.
