# Marketing & Growth

## Goal
Turn a working product into one people find, understand in 5 seconds, try, and pay for.

## Always do
1. **Positioning first**: for [target customer] who [problem], [product] is a [category] that [key benefit], unlike [alternative].
2. **Landing page structure**: clear headline (outcome, not feature) → subhead → primary CTA → proof (logos, numbers, testimonials) → benefits → how it works → objections/FAQ → CTA.
3. **Copy**: customer's words (from research), specific numbers, benefits over features, one idea per section.
4. **SEO basics**: one `h1`, unique title/meta description per page, fast pages, semantic HTML, sitemap, descriptive URLs, structured data.
5. **Analytics plan**: define the funnel (visit → signup → activation → paid → retained) and the events for each step before launch; privacy-compliant.
6. **Activation**: identify the "aha moment" and shorten time-to-value in onboarding.
7. **Pricing**: value-based tiers; enterprise tier with SSO, audit logs, SLA, "Contact sales".
8. **Experiment**: one hypothesis per A/B test, with a success metric and sample size.
9. **Launch plan**: audience, channels, messaging, assets, date, success metrics.
10. **Honesty**: no fake reviews, dark patterns, or unverifiable claims.

## Output
Deliver copy ready to paste, plus the metric each piece is meant to move.
