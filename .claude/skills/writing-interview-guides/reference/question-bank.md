# Interview question bank

Questions grouped by learning objective. Replace bracketed nouns with the product's own terms. Each section ends with bad → good rewrites showing the most common mistakes for that topic.

## Warm-up and wrap-up
- Tell me about your role and what a typical week looks like.
- Who do you work with most closely, inside and outside your team?
- What does a good week look like for you? A bad one?
- (Wrap-up) What is the one thing you would change about how you [do X] today?
- (Wrap-up) Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?
- (Wrap-up) Who else should we talk to about this?

## Workflow
- Walk me through the last time you [completed the task], from the moment you knew you needed to do it.
- What triggered that? What happened right before?
- What did you do next? And after that?
- Where were you working - which screens, files, or tools were open?
- How often does this come up? When was the time before that?
- How did you know you were finished?

| Bad | Problem | Good |
|---|---|---|
| What is your workflow for reports? | Abstract, invites an idealized answer | Walk me through the last report you sent. |
| How do you usually create and share reports? | Double-barreled, "usually" | Tell me about the last report you created. (Then separately:) What happened after it was ready? |

## Pain points and workarounds
- Tell me about a recent time [task] did not go the way you expected.
- What did you do to get around it?
- What did that cost you - time, rework, having to ask someone?
- Have you built anything of your own to handle this - a spreadsheet, a script, a checklist? Can you show me?
- What happens if this goes wrong? Who notices?

| Bad | Problem | Good |
|---|---|---|
| How frustrating is the import process? | Presumes frustration | Tell me about the last time you imported data. |
| What are your biggest pain points? | Jargon, asks for a ranked list from memory | What part of [task] took longer than you expected last time? |
| Is the export slow? | Yes/no, leading | What happened the last time you exported a large file? |

## Tools and integrations
- Which tools did you use for [task] last time? What does each one do for you?
- How does information get from [tool A] to [tool B]?
- When did you last switch tools for this? What prompted it?
- What would break if [tool] disappeared tomorrow?

| Bad | Problem | Good |
|---|---|---|
| Do you use Salesforce with our product? | Yes/no, names the answer | Walk me through where customer data comes from when you set up a new account. |

## Decision making
- Tell me about the last time you had to decide [between options / whether to approve X].
- What information did you need? Where did you get it?
- Who else was involved? Who had the final say?
- What would have made you decide differently?
- How did you explain the decision to others afterward?

| Bad | Problem | Good |
|---|---|---|
| Would better dashboards help you make decisions? | Hypothetical, solution named, yes/no | Tell me about the last decision you made using data from [product]. |

## Buying and procurement
Use with buyers, decision makers and IT/security approvers.
- Tell me about the last software purchase you were involved in for your team.
- How did it start? Who raised the need?
- Who was involved in evaluating it - IT, security, legal, finance?
- What did the security or compliance review ask for (SSO, SOC 2 report, DPA, data residency)?
- What nearly stopped the purchase? How was it resolved?
- How long did it take from first conversation to signed contract?
- How do you judge whether a purchase was worth it a year later?

| Bad | Problem | Good |
|---|---|---|
| How much would you pay for this? | Hypothetical, unreliable | Tell me about how budget was approved for the last tool you bought. |
| Is security important to you? | Yes/no, socially desirable answer | What did your security team ask for the last time you bought software? |

## Onboarding and adoption
- Tell me about your first week using [product]. What did you do first?
- Who helped you get set up? What did they show you?
- When did you feel you could use it on your own? What changed?
- Tell me about someone on your team who stopped using it. What happened?
- How did you roll it out to the rest of your team?

| Bad | Problem | Good |
|---|---|---|
| Was onboarding easy? | Yes/no, leading | Walk me through your first day using [product]. |

## Admin, roles and permissions
Use with workspace admins, IT admins and team leads.
- Walk me through the last time you added or removed someone's access.
- How do you decide who gets which permissions?
- Tell me about a time someone had the wrong access. How did you find out?
- How do you check what changed and who changed it?
- What do you have to report on to security or auditors? How do you produce it?
- How do you handle someone leaving the company?

| Bad | Problem | Good |
|---|---|---|
| Would you want custom roles? | Hypothetical, solution named | Tell me about the last time the existing roles did not fit someone. |
| Do you use SSO and SCIM? | Yes/no, jargon-led | How do new employees get access to [product] today? |

## Collaboration and handoffs
- Tell me about the last time you handed [work] to someone else. How did you do it?
- How did they know it was ready?
- Tell me about a time something got lost or done twice between people.
- How do you know what your teammates are working on?
- How do you get approval before [action]?

| Bad | Problem | Good |
|---|---|---|
| How do you collaborate and communicate with your team? | Double-barreled, abstract | Tell me about the last time you needed a teammate's input on [work]. |

## Stakeholder and expert interviews
Goal: capture assumptions, constraints, and existing evidence. Treat answers as hypotheses to test with users.
- What decision is this research meant to inform for you?
- What do you believe is true about [users/problem] today? What is that based on?
- What have you heard from customers, and from which customers?
- What would success look like in 6 months? How would we measure it?
- What constraints should I know about - technical, legal, contractual, timeline?
- What would surprise you if we found it?
- Who disagrees with you on this, and why?

Experts (domain specialists, consultants): ask for typical cases, edge cases, regulations, and vocabulary; verify claims with users before treating them as evidence.

## Universal probes
- **TEDW:** Tell me more about that · Explain what you mean by "[their word]" · Describe what that looked like · Walk me through it.
- **Sequence:** What happened next? What happened right before?
- **Why chain (up to 5):** Why was that important? Stop at a goal, constraint or emotion.
- **Last time:** Tell me about the last time that happened.
- **Show:** Can you show me where you did that?
- **Mirror:** Repeat their last few words with a rising tone, then wait.
- **Quantify gently:** Roughly how long did that take? How many times this month?
- **Clarify feature requests:** What would that let you do? What do you do today instead?

## Banned patterns

| Pattern | Example | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Hypothetical | Would you use..., Imagine if... | Ask about the last real instance |
| Yes/no | Do you..., Is it..., Have you ever... | Start with How, What, Tell me, Walk me through |
| Leading | Don't you find..., How annoying is... | Remove the presumed feeling or answer |
| Double-barreled | ...and... in one question | Split into two questions |
| Solution pitch | How useful would AI suggestions be? | Ask about the problem the solution addresses |
| Generalizing | What do you usually / typically do? | Anchor to a specific recent instance |
| Self-rating | How tech-savvy are you? | Ask what they did, not how they rate themselves |
| Future intent | Would you pay / switch / recommend? | Ask about past purchases, switches, recommendations |
